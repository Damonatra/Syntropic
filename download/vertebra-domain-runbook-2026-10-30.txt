# Vertebra — domain runbook: what is waiting, what to do on buy day (2026-10-30)
# You have not bought the sending domains yet. Everything that does not need
# them is now BUILT AND PROVEN. This file is the complete list of what to do
# the day you buy — a wiring job, not a research project.

===========================================================================
WHY THE DOMAINS COME LAST — AND WHY THAT IS THE RIGHT ORDER
===========================================================================

A cold-email sending domain is a consumable. Mailbox providers judge every
sender by its volume curve and its bounce rate; a fresh domain pushed hard
in week one looks exactly like a spam farm, because operationally it is
one. So the enclave now treats sending domains the way it treats live
egress generally: as an armed, ledgered, discipline-enforced capability —
and the discipline exists BEFORE the domains do.

Register nothing, buy nothing, until you are ready to walk the ramp the
day the DNS goes live. A domain that sits registered but unwarmed costs
nothing; a domain that warms and then stalls twice costs a week.

===========================================================================
WHAT IS ALREADY BUILT (NO DOMAINS REQUIRED — DONE, TESTED, PROVEN)
===========================================================================

1. SENDING-DOMAIN LEDGER (SendingDomain table + /api/ops/domains)
   Register each domain the day you buy it. One row per domain carries:
   role (sending | transactional), dnsStatus (pending → dns_added →
   verified), status (spare → warming → ready | paused | burned), and the
   warmup clock (warmupStartedAt — stamped the first time you flip to
   warming; pausing and resuming never resets it).

2. THE EGRESS GATE (mailer interlock 3)
   Live egress refuses, with named reasons:
     unregistered_sending_domain  the ledger is the allowlist
     domain_dns_unverified        no DNS, no send — the provider 401s anyway
     domain_warmup_not_started    registered but still spare
     domain_paused / domain_burned operator hold / retired
     warmup_cap_reached N/M       the ramp is the reputation
   Fail-closed: if the gate cannot read the ledger, it refuses.
   The staged sandbox (no RESEND key) is untouched — proof run:
   8/8 verdicts correct (scripts/domain-gate-proof.ts).

3. THE WARMUP SCHEDULE (lib/vertebra/warmup.ts — pure, derived, never stored)
     day  1– 3    5/day      introduce the domain to the filters
     day  4– 6   10/day      first ramp step
     day  7–10   20/day      bounce rate must be ~zero here
     day 11–14   30/day      full-pattern ramp
     day 15+     40/day      plateau per domain — volume comes from owning
                             several warmed domains, not pushing one

4. THE WATCH (domain_warmup on the Ops deck)
   Fires with its math on:
     · dispatch rows referencing an unregistered domain
     · sends through unverified DNS (day-1 mistake, caught same day)
     · warmup cap reached with backlog queued behind it
     · hard-bounce rate 7d ≥ 5% warn / ≥ 10% crit (floor: ≥10 sends)
   The crit case names the action: pause the domain, audit the list
   source, resume only after two clean days.

5. THE BOARD (Ops tab → "Sending domains — egress ledger")
   Register, flip DNS status, start/pause/resume warmup, read the live
   math the gate enforces. Nothing on the board is a parallel truth.

6. THE THREE MAIL DOORS (built earlier this week, domain-independent)
   /api/mail/inbound               canonical HMAC relay door
   /api/providers/resend/inbound   Resend email.received (Svix)
   /api/providers/resend/events    sent/delivered/opened/bounced/complained
   They sit idle until step 5 below points Resend at them.

===========================================================================
BUY-DAY CHECKLIST — IN ORDER, ROUGHLY ONE HOUR OF WORK
===========================================================================

STEP 0 — WHAT TO BUY (the only decision that needs thought)
  · Buy 1–2 SENDING domains that are NOT your main company domain.
    Never send cold outreach from syntropic-os.com — a spam complaint
    against the outreach domain poisons every transactional email the
    desk sends. The main domain goes in the ledger once, role=
    "transactional", and never carries Channel A volume.
  · Pick plausible lookalikes of the main brand (the console mailer
    already renders sender names cleanly): short, .com, no hyphens or
    digits if you can. Age of the domain matters less than the volume
    curve — a new .com warmed honestly beats an old domain pushed hard.
  · Any registrar; turn WHOIS privacy ON; register 1–2 years (providers
    read short registrations as throwaway).
  · Budget note: 2 domains × ~$10/yr is the entire infra cost of this
    step. Everything else is time.

STEP 1 — REGISTER THEM IN THE LEDGER (1 minute each)
  Console → Ops → Sending domains → Register, or:
    curl -X POST http://localhost:3000/api/ops/domains \
      -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
      -d '{"domain":"send.yourbrand2.com","role":"sending"}'

STEP 2 — ADD EACH DOMAIN IN RESEND (dashboard → Domains → Add)
  Resend issues the exact DNS values. Do not hand-write them from this
  file — copy from the dashboard. You will add roughly:
    SPF     TXT  @                v=spf1 include:resend.dev ~all (or amazonses)
    DKIM    TXT/CNAME ×2–3       the resend-issued selector records
    DMARC   TXT  _dmarc           v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:...  ← start at none
    MX      (receiving domain)   Resend's inbound MX for the reply path
  Flip the ledger row: dns → dns_added.

STEP 3 — WAIT FOR VERIFY, THEN FLIP (minutes to a few hours)
  Resend shows "Verified" when the records propagate. Flip the ledger
  row: dns → verified. The gate now admits the domain — as spare.

STEP 4 — START THE WARMUP (the day the DNS verifies, not before)
  Board → start warmup (status → warming; the clock stamps itself).
  Warmup content matters: send to addresses you control or warm inboxes
  first; reply to yourself; mark yourself as not-spam from a test
  account. Volume follows the schedule automatically — the tick refuses
  more than the cap, so you cannot over-send even by accident.

STEP 5 — POINT THE WEBHOOKS (needs a public URL for the console)
  Resend dashboard → Webhooks → Add webhook:
    endpoint  https://<public-console-host>/api/providers/resend/events
    events    email.sent, email.delivered, email.opened,
              email.bounced, email.complained
  Create a second webhook (or same endpoint, second entry):
    endpoint  https://<public-console-host>/api/providers/resend/inbound
    events    email.received
  Signing: set the endpoint's signing secret to the value already in
  .env (RESEND_WEBHOOK_SECRET) — Svix lets you supply your own secret;
  that keeps the enclave's .env the single source of truth. If you let
  Resend generate one instead, paste it into .env and never into code.

STEP 6 — THE INBOUND ROUTE (so replies have somewhere to land)
  Resend dashboard → Receiving → create route for the domain → deliver
  to the /api/providers/resend/inbound webhook. Requires the domain
  added in step 2 and the MX record from step 2 live.

STEP 7 — FIRST LIVE SEND, THEN LEAVE IT ALONE
  Queue one Channel A dispatch (or let the tick claim one). It should
  pass the gate and leave the building. Watch the Ops deck: the
  resend_events pulses and the domain_warmup watch are the honest
  record of what happened next. Do not touch the ramp. Day 8+ you can
  point real prospect volume at it.

REALISTIC TIMELINE FROM BUY CLICK TO FULL VOLUME
  day 0    buy, ledger, Resend domain, DNS records        (~1 hour of work)
  day 0–2  DNS propagation + Resend verify                (waiting)
  day 1    warmup starts (5/day) — counted from verified day
  day 8    20/day — first meaningful prospect volume
  day 15   40/day/domain plateau; add the second domain's
           ramp in parallel for 80/day combined
  Speeding this up is the one mistake this whole layer exists to make
  impossible.

WHAT STAYS BLOCKED UNTIL BUY DAY (AND WHAT DOES NOT)
===========================================================================

BLOCKED ON DOMAINS (the list above — nothing else):
  · any live Channel A send (the gate refuses unledgered domains)
  · Resend webhook pointing (needs a live verified domain for events
    to exist; the doors themselves are proven with synthetic payloads)
  · real inbound replies (no MX, no replies)

NOT BLOCKED — buildable any time, no domains needed:
  · Tier 2 fuel: contact resolver (needs an Apollo/Hunter key, not a
    domain), pain-signal harvester, calendar connector (needs OAuth)
  · Tier 3 reach: OutboxEvent signed publisher, real pager (Slack/
    Twilio tokens), CRM mirror, nightly ledger snapshot
  · rail minimize/maximize icon (still parked, needs your go)

When you buy: do steps 1–7 in order, then tell me and I will run the
wiring verification (DNS dig checks, webhook handshake test, one gated
live send) end to end.
