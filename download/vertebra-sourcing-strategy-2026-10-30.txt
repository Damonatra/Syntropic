VERTERBA SOURCING STRATEGY — HOW PROSPECTS REACH THE LEDGER
2026-10-30 · companion to the domain-day runbook · state: 191/191 tests, keys staged, sourcing spoke pending

THE ONE-LINE STRATEGY
Vertebra never buys a list. Prospects are earned through four doors into one
ledger; money (reveal credits, sends) is spent only on leads that clear the
QLV bar the code already enforces (CHANNEL_A_MIN_SCORE = 70).

THE ICP — READ FROM THE SCORING BRAIN, NOT INVENTED
The six QLV factors define who a prospect IS:
  rfq_latency 0.28 | legacy_portal 0.22 | unapplied_spa 0.18 |
  manual_quoting_share 0.14 | vertical_fit 0.10 | size_fit 0.08
Profile: industrial distribution (PVF / electrical / HVAC / bearings) running
Prophet 21, Infor or Eclipse, quoting through manual_mailto, static_pdf_form
or legacy_portal channels. quotePortalType = modern_quote_api is a NON-prospect
— they already solved it. 18% of the weight (vertical_fit + size_fit) is
scoreable before any contact: pre-contact scoring is real, not vibes.

DOOR 1 — PAIN SIGNALS ($0, feeds every opener)          [BUILT, needs cadence]
PainSignal rows: job_posting ("hiring inside sales / quote desk"),
review_complaint, site_copy, audit_finding, research. The `verbatim` field is
the exact words that make the email land. Operator cadence: 30 min/week,
harvest → attach to prospect rows. No credits, no sends — just texture.

DOOR 2 — PORTAL AUDIT INGEST (warmest door)             [BUILT]
A distributor uploads quotes to the portal dropzone → AuditDocument parsed →
findings scored. A prospect who handed over their own data outranks any
purchased contact. This door is the product-led engine: audit → margin
recovery report → pilot engagement.

DOOR 3 — APOLLO SOURCING SEARCH (the one missing spoke) [BUILT 2026-10-31]
Bulk search by ICP filters (Apollo mixed_people/search — search itself burns
no email credits) → staged Prospect rows (source:"sourcing", deduped via
@@unique([source, domainKey])) → pre-contact fit rubric (vertical .35 ·
erp .20 · size .15 · region .15 · title .15; reveal bar 70 mirrors
CHANNEL_A_MIN_SCORE) → only fit ≥ 70 enter the reveal queue → EXISTING
resolver (name+domain → email, registry-gated, ledgered) → verify →
CASL + suppression gate → dispatch queue, warmup-gated. Panel: Ops →
"Sourcing — the prospect spoke" (staging batch / live batch / math per
row / reveal / dismiss; dismissed verdicts survive re-staging). Staging
batches run .example.com fixtures while the key is absent — they queue and
score honestly but never reveal; a reveal walks the resolver's own gate and
refuses with its named reason.

DOOR 4 — INBOUND (already built)                        [BUILT]
Portal form, mail relay, replies. Every dispatch manufactures inbound:
replies classify → interested / objection → high-touch → dossier →
engagement. Highest intent, zero cost.

THE BAR (fail-closed, in order)
dedupe (@@unique[source, domainKey]) → suppression check → CASL scope →
QLV pre-score → [reveal credits ONLY if band ≥ high] → email verify →
dispatch (warmup cap + egress gate). Any missing link refuses with a NAMED
reason; nothing skips silently.

THE HONEST $0 MATH
  Apollo free: 900 reveal credits/yr ≈ 75/mo. Hunter: 50 verifies/mo.
  Warmup: 5 → 40/day ⇒ month-1 send capacity ≈ 400–500 total.
  At 2–4% reply on a tight ICP: 1–3 conversations in month one.
Month one validates copy, ICP and deliverability — not revenue.
SCALE TRIGGER: ≥3% reply rate AND <2% bounce for two consecutive weeks →
then buy credits (~$49/mo tier) or add a second warmed domain. Paying
before that signal is how cold-email budgets die.

THE SEQUENCE (3 touches, 9 days, once warmup allows)
  Day 1: opener referencing the PainSignal verbatim (≤90 words, plain text,
         one link max). Day 4: one-line bump. Day 9: proof / peer number.
Personalization comes from Door 1, never mail-merge tokens.

THE LOOP THAT PAYS FOR ITSELF
sends → Resend events (bounce/complaint → suppression) → replies → mail door
→ triage → upweight the signals that correlated with replies. Spend flat,
score smarter weekly.
